# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Between:**
- **Data Controller:** [Customer Company Name]
- **Data Processor:** BoardingPass, Inc.

**Effective Date:** [Date]

## 1. Definitions

- **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on Personal Data
- **Data Subject:** The individual whose Personal Data is being processed
- **GDPR:** General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679

## 2. Scope and Purpose

BoardingPass will process Personal Data on behalf of the Customer solely for the purpose of providing client onboarding services as described in the Service Agreement.

## 3. Data Processor Obligations

BoardingPass agrees to:
- Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer
- Ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Assist the Customer in responding to Data Subject requests
- Delete or return Personal Data upon termination of services

## 4. Security Measures

BoardingPass implements the following security measures:
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Row-Level Security (RLS) policies
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and authentication
- Audit logging
- Incident response procedures

## 5. Sub-processors

Current sub-processors:
1. Supabase (Database & Storage)
2. Vercel (Hosting)
3. Resend (Email Delivery)

The Customer consents to the use of these sub-processors. BoardingPass will notify the Customer 30 days before adding new sub-processors.

## 6. Data Subject Rights

BoardingPass will assist the Customer in responding to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under GDPR, including:
- Right to access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure
- Right to data portability
- Right to object

## 7. Data Breach Notification

BoardingPass will notify the Customer within 72 hours of becoming aware of any Personal Data breach.

## 8. Audits and Compliance

The Customer may audit BoardingPass's compliance with this DPA once per year with 30 days' notice.

## 9. Data Deletion

Upon termination, BoardingPass will delete all Personal Data within 90 days unless legal obligations require retention.

## 10. Liability

Liability is limited as specified in the Service Agreement.
